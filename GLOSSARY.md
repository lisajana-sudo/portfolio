# Portfolio

The public page for Lisa Jana, a biotechnology student working where biology meets AI.

## Language

**Home**:
The opening view: her name, a short introduction, and the helix.
_Avoid_: Hero, landing

**About**:
The biography, the three counts beside it, and the quote.
_Avoid_: Bio

**Disciplines**:
The groups of practice — computation, the bench, applied biotechnology, and earth repair.
_Avoid_: Skills

**Journey**:
The sequence of roles and studies.
_Avoid_: Experience, resume, timeline

**Work**:
The selected projects.
_Avoid_: Portfolio, case studies

**Contact**:
How to reach her: the email and the social links.
_Avoid_: Footer
